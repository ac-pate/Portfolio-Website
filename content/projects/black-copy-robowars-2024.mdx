## Overview

Black Copy was my first robotics project — and the one that changed everything. Built entirely solo for Robowars 2024, this autonomous sumo robot marked my entry into the world of robotics, embedded systems, and competitive engineering.

In a competition where teams are the norm, I took on every aspect myself: mechanical design, electronics, and programming. It was ambitious, chaotic, and exactly what I needed.

## Technical Details

### Mechanical Design
- Complete CAD design from scratch
- 3D printed chassis and components
- First experience with design-for-manufacturing principles

### Electronics
- Motor driver integration and wiring
- Power distribution system
- Sensor mounting and cable management

### Software
- Microcontroller programming in C++
- PWM control for motor speed regulation
- Basic autonomous behavior logic

## What I Learned

This project was a crash course in:
- **Motor drivers**: How H-bridges work, PWM duty cycles, and current handling
- **Microcontrollers**: GPIO, timers, interrupts, and real-time control
- **CAD & 3D Printing**: Tolerances, print orientation, and iterative design
- **Systems thinking**: How mechanical, electrical, and software interact

## Outcome

Competed at Robowars 2024 and placed 4th. More importantly, I walked away knowing that robotics was my path forward.
