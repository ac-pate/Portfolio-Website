## Overview

IEEEXtreme is a global 24-hour programming competition organized by IEEE. Teams of students solve increasingly difficult algorithmic problems, competing against thousands of teams worldwide.

## Format

- **Duration**: 24 hours continuous
- **Problems**: Released progressively throughout the competition
- **Scoring**: Points based on problem difficulty and solve time
- **Global**: Simultaneous participation across all time zones

## My Experience

This was technically my first hackathon — 24 hours of coding, problem-solving, and caffeine. I competed with a team, primarily using Python and C++.

At the time, competitive programming was still new to me, so I was more of a learner than a heavy contributor. But the experience was valuable:
- First exposure to the intensity of overnight coding marathons
- Learned how teams divide and conquer algorithmic problems
- Built endurance for future hackathons and competitions

## Languages Used

- Python (primary)
- C++ (for performance-critical problems)

## Takeaway

IEEEXtreme taught me that I enjoy the pressure and camaraderie of timed competitions — a realization that carried into every hardware hackathon and robotics competition that followed.
