## Overview

Robowars is IEEE Concordia's annual autonomous sumo robot competition. Robots face off in a ring — the goal is to push your opponent out or be the last one standing.

## My Entry: Black Copy

This was my first robotics project and my first competition. While most teams had 2-4 members, I built Black Copy entirely solo:
- Complete mechanical design and CAD
- All electronics and wiring
- Embedded programming and control logic

## Result

**4th Place** — Not bad for a first attempt with zero prior robotics experience.

## Why It Mattered

This competition sparked my interest in robotics. Walking into Robowars 2024, I was a computer engineering student interested in chip manufacturing. Walking out, I was hooked on making things move.

Black Copy was chaotic, imperfect, and exactly what I needed. It led directly to everything that came after — more sumo bots, IEEE projects, research internships, and eventually a robotics-focused career path.
